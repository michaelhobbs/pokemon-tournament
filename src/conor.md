# Game plan: Michael (7) vs Conor (9) — Second Half

Level 100 Doubles Custom Game, pick 4 from 6, all three games played. One Tera per battle. Michael's six sets are locked for all three games; he can change which 4 and leads each game, but not the sets. Conor's exact abilities/items/moves/EVs/natures/Tera are unknown, so this is a read, not confirmed scouting.

**Important:** `src/data/pokemon-sets.ts` is only for website gym battles — not tournament scouting.

## Recommendation

**Default 4:** Tyranitar, Sableye, Excadrill, Greninja.
**Bench:** Scizor and Gardevoir while Chandelure is available.

**Lead:** Sableye + Tyranitar. If Gyarados is on the field, Tera Fairy Tyranitar immediately. If you do not want to commit Tera on turn 1, lead Sableye + Excadrill instead and keep Tyranitar in back for Chandelure.

## Conor's likely plan

Conor's team is built around **Gyarados + Chandelure + Milotic + Raichu**, with Cloyster/Meowstic as the flex slots.

- **Raichu's Lightning Rod** protects Gyarados and Milotic from Electric moves.
- **Gyarados** is the physical win condition; expect Choice Band, Dragon Dance, or Scarf.
- **Chandelure** is the anti-Scizor/anti-Gardevoir breaker; expect Specs or Scarf.
- **Milotic** walls, burns with Scald, and stalls with Recover.
- **Cloyster** is the Shell Smash win condition if he brings it.
- **Meowstic** is the Prankster support/control option; it is faster than Sableye.

## Why these sets

- **Tyranitar:** Assault Vest + Sand Stream lets it tank Chandelure, Raichu, and Milotic while setting sand for Excadrill. Crunch OHKOs Chandelure; Rock Slide pressures Gyarados and Chandelure together; High Horsepower answers Raichu; Thunder Punch is for Milotic/Cloyster once Raichu is gone.
- **Excadrill:** Sand Rush is the key. In sand it outspeeds Scarf Chandelure and +2 Cloyster. Life Orb gives it the power to revenge kill. Protect is important in doubles.
- **Sableye:** Prankster WoW/Taunt shuts down Gyarados, Cloyster, and Milotic. Meowstic is faster and can set Safeguard/screens before Taunt, but Meowstic cannot Taunt Sableye. Leftovers > Safety Goggles because Conor has no sleep moves.
- **Greninja:** Fast offensive check to Chandelure and Meowstic. Dark Pulse OHKOs Chandelure; Grass Knot 2HKOs Milotic and punishes Tera Water/Ground Gyarados; U-turn keeps momentum.
- **Gardevoir:** Bench by default because Chandelure and Raichu make it risky. Bring it only when Chandelure is gone/benched; then Thunderbolt pressures Gyarados/Milotic and Moonblast handles Tera Dark/Fighting targets.
- **Scizor:** Bench by default because Chandelure's Fire move is a 4x OHKO. Tera Water is only a panic button to survive one surprise Fire hit; it is not a reason to bring Scizor into Chandelure.

## Tera plan

**Primary: Tyranitar → Fairy.**
Against Conor's roster it has **no exploitable weakness** — only Steel/Poison hit it, and Conor has neither. It turns Gyarados's Waterfall, Milotic's Scald, Raichu's Focus Blast, and Ground/Grass coverage into neutral or resisted hits. It keeps Crunch/Rock Slide STAB. Use it when Gyarados threatens Tyranitar or when Raichu's Focus Blast would otherwise be 4x.

**Backup: Greninja → Dark** to guarantee Dark Pulse damage and blank Psychic.
**Backup: Excadrill → Ground** only late-game when Water/Ice threats are gone.
**Emergency: Gardevoir → Fairy** if Chandelure Teras Fighting/Dark.
**Emergency: Scizor → Water** only to survive a surprise Chandelure Fire move.
**Emergency: Sableye → Fairy** removes its only weakness, but Tera is usually better spent on Tyranitar.

## Critical corrections and rules

- **Sableye is not a Chandelure answer.** It is neutral to Chandelure's STABs but too frail specially. Tyranitar, Greninja, and Excadrill are the Chandelure answers.
- **Do not click Electric moves while Raichu is on the field.** Lightning Rod redirects Thunder Punch/Thunderbolt and gives Raichu +1 SpA.
- **Do not lead Tyranitar into Water without Tera Fairy.** Choice Band Gyarados Waterfall can OHKO it.
- **Chandelure is faster than Greninja if Scarf.** Tyranitar still tanks it; Excadrill in sand revenge kills.
- **Meowstic is faster Prankster than Sableye, but cannot Taunt Sableye (Dark).** Do not expect Sableye's Taunt to beat Meowstic's first screen/Trick Room, but Sableye's own support is reliable. Kill Meowstic with Greninja/Tyranitar.
- **Milotic may have Competitive.** Avoid bringing Gardevoir in with Trace/Intimidate while Milotic is out. Avoid WoW if it has Marvel Scale.
- **Cloyster may have Overcoat.** Sand will not break its Sash then. Taunt it before Shell Smash.

## Adaptation rules

- **If Conor benches Chandelure:** bring Gardevoir over Greninja. If Cloyster is the main problem, bring Scizor instead.
- **If Conor leads Gyarados + Raichu:** Tera Fairy Tyranitar, Rock Slide, and WoW/Taunt with Sableye. If you want to preserve Tera, lead Sableye + Excadrill instead.
- **If Conor leads Chandelure + Milotic:** lead Sableye + Tyranitar; Crunch Chandelure and Taunt Milotic.
- **If Conor uses Cloyster:** Taunt first, WoW it, and use Sand to break Sash; Scizor is a strong bring only if Chandelure is absent.
- **If Conor uses Trick Room:** lean on Tyranitar/Sableye/Scizor and avoid Excadrill/Greninja, or remove Meowstic immediately.
- **If Chandelure Teras Fairy:** switch from Crunch/Dark Pulse to Excadrill Iron Head or Scizor Bullet Punch.
- **If Chandelure Teras Fighting/Dark:** Gardevoir Tera Fairy Moonblast is the emergency answer.
- **If Gyarados Teras Water/Ground:** Greninja Grass Knot. **If Steel:** High Horsepower. **If Fairy:** Iron Head/Bullet Punch.
- **If Milotic Teras Fairy:** Iron Head/Bullet Punch. **If Steel:** High Horsepower/Close Combat.
- **If Raichu Teras Fairy:** Iron Head. **If Flying:** Rock Slide.

**Win condition:** burn/neutralize Gyarados, remove Chandelure with Tyranitar or Greninja, then let sand + Excadrill clean up. Don't try to win through Chandelure with Scizor or Gardevoir.
