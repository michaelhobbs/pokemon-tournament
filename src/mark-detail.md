## 3. Watch Outs & Sequencing Tips

These are the little things that win or lose this matchup. Pay extra attention to these!

### Abilities & Mechanics To Watch For

- **Neutralizing Gas (Weezing)** - When Weezing switches in, every ability on the field is disabled for as long as Weezing is active. This means Scizor's Technician will NOT boost Bullet Punch/U-Turn, and any other ability reliant things will be nullified. Do not switch Scizor into this.
- **Spore (Amoonguss)** - This is a sleep move with no accuracy checks in practice for the purpose of it being scary. If your Pokemon falls asleep, you're going to be wasting turns. Pivot with U-Turn or just switch out before it gets to click it if you can predict it.
- **Aurora Veil (Ninetales)** - For 5 turns, every hit is halved. This does NOT mean you lose, it means you switch to a chip game. Sand will still chip every turn whilst Veil is up, so focus on breaking the mons behind it, not breaking Ninetales in one hit.
- **Truant (Slaking)** - Every other turn, it does nothing. If it just used Return and you almost died, don't revenge kill it in a panic, just heal up your positioning and hit it on its next turn when it's loafing.
- **Natural Cure (Blissey)** - If Blissey switches out whilst statused, its status will be cured. This is why _Taunting_ it is astronomically better than trying to burn/paralyze it. If it's taunted, it cannot heal at all, preventing this from ever being relevant.

### Sequencing Advice (How To Play The Battle)

1. **Lead Correctly** - Lead Tyranitar almost every game 1. Set sand immediately. This is the foundation for the entire matchup.
2. **Save Disruption** - Keep Sableye in the back until Blissey or Weezing come out. Don't waste your Prankster Taunt on something that doesn't need it, save it for Soft-Boiled or for something that is about to be very annoying.
3. **Pivot Religiously** - Scizor's U-Turn is your "get out of jail free" card. If you even THINK you're going to get spored, or if Gas is about to come in, U-Turn out of it. Do not tank things that you can safely pivot away from.
4. **Fix Hazards Instantly** - If you see Stealth Rock/Sticky Web go up, get Excadrill out next and Rapid Spin. The chip adds up incredibly fast for this team and will make trades a lot worse for you.
5. **Play The Long Game** - This is a chip matchup, not a sweep. Between Sand, Rocky Helmet (from Sableye), and chipping, you will win by wearing them down. Do not get greedy trying to KO something untaunted Blissey, bait it out and chip it down.

### Tera Timing Examples

- **Sableye Vs Blissey/Ninetales** - If Blissey is about to click a move into Sableye and you need to guarantee it lives to taunt again or to tank a hit whilst disrupted, Tera Ghost is perfect for this.
- **Scizor Vs A Scary Hit** - If you're forced to take a Moonblast from Ninetales or a big hit that would otherwise KO Scizor, Tera Steel to live and then U-Turn out of it.
- **Tyranitar For The Break** - After getting Blissey down to a sliver via sand and chip, Tera Dark Tyranitar and click Crunch to try and break it when it would have otherwise lived. Only do this if the KO would win you the trade/end the mon.

## Final Quick Reminder

- **Bring:** Scizor, Excadrill, Tyranitar, Sableye
- **Lead:** Tyranitar to set Sand
- **Disrupt:** Sableye Taunt Blissey FIRST whenever it comes out
- **Safety:** Never switch Scizor into Weezing whilst Neutralizing Gas is up, never leave your sweepers in front of Amoonguss
- **Cleanup:** Excadrill for hazards, Scizor for pivots and revenge kills
- **Tera:** Save your 1 tera per battle, Sableye Ghost is your most valuable one for this matchup.

This matchup is won by patience. Chip, pivot, and disrupt. If you play those three things correctly, you'll be in a fantastic spot against Mark!
