# Michael vs Conor — Final Tips

A few higher-level edges beyond the turn script:

- **Scout the 4, not the 6.** Conor has two flex slots. Identify whether he brought Cloyster or Meowstic, and whether Chandelure/Raichu are present. No Chandelure → Gardevoir/Scizor become safe. No Raichu → Electric moves unlock. No Milotic → Greninja is less necessary.
- **Game 1 is information.** All three games are played. Track Conor's items, abilities, Tera types, leads, and which 4 he brings. Don't reveal Tera unless it prevents a KO—except Tera Fairy Tyranitar vs Gyarados, which is worth showing.
- **Don't overpredict switches.** Spread Rock Slide and safe pressure beat fancy reads. If you WoW Gyarados and he switches to Chandelure, WoW fails unless Chandelure has already Terastallized Fighting. Rock Slide is the stable turn 1.
- **Tera discipline.** Default is Tyranitar → Fairy. Don't Tera Greninja, Excadrill, Gardevoir, or Scizor unless it directly wins the turn or game. Tera Fairy Tyranitar is still excellent even if Gyarados switches out.
- **Protect your anchor and cleaner.** Tyranitar is your Chandelure/Raichu answer—don't trade it for Milotic. Excadrill is your cleaner—don't bring it in early just because sand is up. Sableye is support—don't sack it while Gyarados or Cloyster is alive.
- **Respect Scald and burn.** Milotic's Scald can cripple Tyranitar/Excadrill. Switch Greninja in on Milotic—it resists Water—and force it out with Grass Knot. Don't WoW Milotic if it has Marvel Scale; don't Trace Intimidate if it has Competitive.
- **Play around Fake Out and Taunt.** Raichu/Meowstic cannot Fake Out Sableye (Ghost), and Meowstic cannot Taunt it (Dark). Sableye's support is reliable; Meowstic's edge is being faster with Safeguard/screens. Don't expect to flinch or Taunt Sableye—Tyranitar's Rock Slide/Crunch is the stable action.
- **Trick Room alert.** If Meowstic sets Trick Room, Tyranitar/Sableye/Scizor benefit, but Excadrill/Greninja become liabilities. Next game, adjust the 4 or remove Meowstic immediately.
- **Expect adaptation.** If you win with Tera Fairy Tyranitar, Conor may Tera Chandelure Fairy or Tera Ground/Steel Gyarados. Have Scizor Tera Water/Excadrill Iron Head and Greninja Grass Knot ready. Don't reuse a losing lead.
- **Practice and log.** Test the exact six on Showdown against Conor's likely leads. Between games, write down his items, Tera, leads, and what killed you. Take safe lines—don't chase 90% Rock Slides or unnecessary Tera.

Biggest overall edge: **neutralize Gyarados with WoW/Taunt, remove Chandelure, then let sand + Excadrill clean up.** Don't try to out-water Conor or win through Chandelure with Scizor/Gardevoir.
