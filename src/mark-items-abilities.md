# Mark Items and Abilities

Full glossary for the Mark matchup. Under Gen 9 doubles mechanics,
**Neutralizing Gas suppresses all abilities except its own**; an **Ability Shield**
on a Pokémon blocks that suppression.

## Abilities

| Ability          | What it does                                                                                                           | Battle relevance                                                                                                           |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Neutralizing Gas | While this Weezing is on the field, every other Pokémon's ability is suppressed.                                       | Turns off Truant on Slaking, so it attacks every turn. Also turns off Prankster/Technician/Sand Stream.                    |
| Prankster        | Status moves get +1 priority.                                                                                          | Sableye's Taunt/WoW/Recover. Gas removes the priority; Sableye's 136 Speed then matters.                                   |
| Technician       | Boosts moves with 60 BP or less by 1.5x.                                                                               | Scizor's Bullet Punch. Gas removes the boost; it is still 4x on Ninetales and 2x on Weezing.                               |
| Sand Stream      | Sets sandstorm on entry for 5 turns. Sand chips non-Rock/Ground/Steel by 1/16 and boosts Rock-type SpD by 1.5x.        | Tyranitar. Gas suppresses it. When Gas ends, it restarts; because Tyranitar is slower than Ninetales, sand overrides snow. |
| Sand Rush        | Doubles Speed in sandstorm.                                                                                            | Mentioned for comparison: our Excadrill uses Mold Breaker, so it gets no sand Speed boost.                                 |
| Mold Breaker     | The user's moves ignore the target's ability.                                                                          | Excadrill's High Horsepower hits Levitate Weezing and bypasses Shuckle's Sturdy. Gas suppresses Mold Breaker.              |
| Trace            | On entry, copies a random adjacent foe's ability.                                                                      | Gardevoir. Can copy Snow Warning (sets snow, may enable Veil) or Truant (bad). Cannot copy Neutralizing Gas.               |
| Protean          | Changes the user's type to the move's type for STAB. In Gen 9, only once per switch-in.                                | Greninja's first move gets STAB and changes its defensive type; then it stays that type until it switches.                 |
| Snow Warning     | Sets snow on entry for 5 turns, 8 with Icy Rock. Snow boosts Ice-type Defense by 1.5x and enables Aurora Veil.         | Ninetales. Gas suppresses it. When Gas ends, it restarts; sand can then override it.                                       |
| Snow Cloak       | Raises evasion in snow.                                                                                                | Ninetales alternative. It does not set snow, so it cannot use Aurora Veil by itself.                                       |
| Levitate         | Immune to Ground moves and grounded hazards.                                                                           | Weezing alternative. Mold Breaker ignores it; Gas suppresses it.                                                           |
| Misty Surge      | Sets Misty Terrain on entry for 5 turns. Grounded Pokémon cannot be statused or confused, and Dragon damage is halved. | Weezing alternative. Blocks WoW on grounded Slaking. Mutually exclusive with Neutralizing Gas.                             |
| Regenerator      | Heals 1/3 max HP when switching out.                                                                                   | Amoonguss. Gas suppresses it, so it may not heal if it leaves while Weezing is out.                                        |
| Effect Spore     | 30% chance to poison, paralyze, or sleep on contact.                                                                   | Amoonguss. Gas suppresses it. Safety Goggles do **not** block it.                                                          |
| Sturdy           | Survives a damaging hit at 1 HP from full HP; also blocks OHKO moves.                                                  | Shuckle. Gas and Mold Breaker bypass it.                                                                                   |
| Contrary         | Reverses stat changes.                                                                                                 | Shuckle alternative. Lowering its stats raises them; Gas suppresses it.                                                    |
| Truant           | Slaking loafs every other turn.                                                                                        | Gas suppresses it, so Slaking attacks every turn. Trace can copy it, which is bad for Gardevoir.                           |
| Natural Cure     | Cures status when switching out.                                                                                       | Blissey. Gas suppresses it.                                                                                                |
| Serene Grace     | Doubles the chance of secondary effects.                                                                               | Blissey. Gas suppresses it; Ice Beam/Flame Thrower freeze/burn chances double while active.                                |
| Healer           | 30% chance to cure an ally's status at the end of each turn.                                                           | Blissey. Doubles only. Gas suppresses it.                                                                                  |
| Gluttony         | Eats pinch Berries at 50% HP instead of 25%.                                                                           | Shuckle's third possible ability. Only matters if it holds a pinch Berry, not Sitrus.                                      |

## Items

| Item           | What it does                                                                                    | Battle relevance                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choice Band    | 1.5x Attack; locks the holder into its first move until it switches.                            | Scizor; Slaking possible. Bullet Punch lock is fine; switching resets it.                                   |
| Choice Specs   | 1.5x Special Attack; locks the holder into its first move.                                      | Gardevoir. Pick the right move; it is locked until it switches.                                             |
| Choice Scarf   | 1.5x Speed; locks the holder into its first move.                                               | Slaking/Ninetales/Weezing/Blissey possible. Scarf Slaking reaches 448/492 Speed.                            |
| Assault Vest   | 1.5x Special Defense, but prevents the holder from using status moves.                          | Tyranitar. All of its moves are damaging.                                                                   |
| Life Orb       | 1.3x damage; holder loses 10% max HP after each damaging attack.                                | Greninja; Slaking possible. The recoil adds up.                                                             |
| Leftovers      | Heals 1/16 max HP at the end of each turn.                                                      | Excadrill; Blissey/Shuckle possible.                                                                        |
| Safety Goggles | Blocks powder moves (Spore, Rage Powder, etc.) and weather chip damage.                         | Sableye. Does not block Effect Spore, WoW, or Toxic.                                                        |
| Light Clay     | Extends Aurora Veil/Reflect/Light Screen from 5 to 8 turns.                                     | Ninetales. Does not extend snow; removing it after Veil does not shorten the current Veil.                  |
| Icy Rock       | Extends snow/hail from 5 to 8 turns.                                                            | Ninetales. Does not extend Aurora Veil; removing it after snow does not shorten the current snow.           |
| Focus Sash     | Survives one damaging hit at 1 HP if the holder is at full HP; consumed after use.              | Ninetales. Sand chip or a multi-hit move can still finish it.                                               |
| Rocky Helmet   | Contact attacker loses 1/6 max HP.                                                              | Weezing/Amoonguss possible. Bullet Punch, U-turn, Knock Off, and Rapid Spin all trigger it.                 |
| Mental Herb    | Single-use; cures Taunt, Encore, Torment, Disable, Heal Block, and infatuation.                 | Shuckle. Knock Off first, then Taunt.                                                                       |
| Black Sludge   | Poison types heal 1/16 per turn; non-Poison types lose 1/8 per turn.                            | Weezing/Amoonguss. Knock Off removes their healing.                                                         |
| Sitrus Berry   | Single-use; restores 1/4 max HP when the holder is at 50% HP or less.                           | Weezing/Amoonguss/Blissey/Shuckle. Knock Off removes it before it can be eaten.                             |
| Ability Shield | Prevents the holder's ability from being suppressed or changed by Neutralizing Gas/Gastro Acid. | On Slaking it would keep Truant and block the Gas combo, but Slaking then cannot hold Choice Band/Life Orb. |

**Key reminders:** Knock Off removes any of these items; removing Light Clay or
Icy Rock after activation does **not** shorten the current screen/weather. Choice
items lock moves, Assault Vest blocks status moves, and Safety Goggles do not stop
Effect Spore.
