# Michael vs Mark — Noob-Friendly Guide

**Best noob-friendly win: kill Weezing first. If Neutralizing Gas is up, Slaking has no Truant (unless it holds Ability Shield) and can attack every turn.**

## Bring

- Blind/default four: **Tyranitar / Sableye / Scizor / Gardevoir**.
- Ninetales and Shuckle both in Mark's four: **Tyranitar / Sableye / Scizor / Excadrill**.
- Shuckle in the four but no Ninetales: **Sableye / Scizor / Gardevoir / Excadrill**.
- If you only see Mark's six, use the blind four unless you expect Shuckle; then use the appropriate Shuckle four above.

## Lead

- Ninetales in the four: **Tyranitar + Sableye**.
- Slaking in the four, no Ninetales: **Sableye + Scizor**.
- Otherwise: **Sableye + Gardevoir**.
- Exception: if you expect **Gas + Slaking on the lead** and brought Tyranitar, use **Tyranitar + Gardevoir**; otherwise lead **Scizor + Gardevoir**. Bring Sableye after Weezing is gone.

## Turn 1

- **Tyranitar:** Rock Slide. If Ninetales is active, aim at it. Sand clears snow (or Gas stops both weather abilities), blocking turn-1 Aurora Veil.
- **Sableye:**
  - Slaking active, no Misty Terrain, and Gas is not up → **Will-O-Wisp Slaking**.
  - Slaking active and Gas is up → **do not default to WoW**. Choice Band Play Rough OHKOs Sableye before it moves; Life Orb leaves it at 8 HP. Knock Off Weezing or switch Sableye out; kill Weezing first.
  - Weezing active and Gas is up, with no Slaking active → **Knock Off Weezing** or switch Sableye out.
  - Amoonguss active → **Taunt Amoonguss**.
  - Shuckle active → **Taunt Shuckle**, unless it has Mental Herb; then Knock Off first.
  - Otherwise → **Taunt** the support Pokémon.
- **If Scizor is your lead:** Bullet Punch Slaking or Weezing; U-turn if Fire Punch is likely.
- **If Gardevoir is your lead:** Psychic Weezing/Amoonguss, Psyshock Blissey, or Moonblast Shuckle; don't expect a KO through Sturdy.

## Turn 2 onward

- **Gas Weezing is the emergency.** Kill it with Gardevoir Psychic, Scizor Bullet Punch, Excadrill High Horsepower, or Tyranitar Rock Slide. Until it dies, ignore plans based on Prankster, Technician, Sand Stream, Mold Breaker, or Trace.
- **When Gas Weezing is gone,** Truant applies again (or never left, if Slaking holds Ability Shield). Attack Slaking on its loafing turn; don't waste those turns.
- **Slaking:** WoW it if terrain allows and Gas is not up. Never leave Sableye in on Play Rough. Sableye has **no Protect**; use Recover, Knock Off, or switch, and let its partner use Protect. Even burned, do not switch Tyranitar or Excadrill into Choice Band Earthquake. Only click Tyranitar Superpower on a Truant turn.
- **Amoonguss:** Taunt it. Safety Goggles protect **only Sableye**; it can still Spore the partner.
- **Shuckle:** Knock Off first, then Taunt. Bring Excadrill to Rapid Spin after hazards. Mold Breaker ignores Sturdy, but Shuckle is still bulky enough that a KO is not guaranteed.
- **Ninetales:** if sand is gone, Taunt it before Aurora Veil. Safety Goggles do not block Hypnosis.
- **Weezing:** Taunt it before Toxic Spikes, or Rapid Spin them away.

## Never do these

- Stall Truant while Neutralizing Gas is up; there is no Truant to stall unless Slaking holds Ability Shield.
- Leave Sableye in on Slaking's Play Rough.
- Assume Sableye has Protect.
- Assume Goggles protect Gardevoir or Scizor from Spore.
- Expect Mold Breaker to guarantee a Shuckle KO.
- Tera Rock into Weezing and expect Rock Slide to become super effective.
- Keep Scizor in on Fire Punch, or lock it into a move you will need later.
- Leave Greninja in after Protean changes it to a type weak to Fire Punch.
- Switch Gardevoir in blind if Trace could copy Snow Warning, Misty Surge, or Truant.

**Extra tip:** if Weezing is not on the field, Sableye is your safe button. If Weezing is on the field, ask "is Gas up?" before trusting Sableye. When in doubt, kill Weezing.
