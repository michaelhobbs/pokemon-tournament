# Michael vs Conor — Noob-Friendly Guide

**Best noob-friendly win: play the sand core, not the Gardevoir line.**

Bring **Tyranitar / Sableye / Excadrill / Greninja**. Lead **Sableye + Tyranitar**. Your goal is not to outpredict Conor—it's to remove Chandelure's Scarf, neutralize Gyarados, keep sand up, and let Excadrill clean.

## Turn 1 script

- **Sableye:** Knock Off Chandelure. If no Chandelure, Knock Off Gyarados's Lum Berry or Meowstic's Sitrus Berry.
- **Tyranitar:** Tera Fairy if Chandelure or Gyarados is on the field, then click Rock Slide.
- Fake Out cannot stop Tyranitar's Tera. If Chandelure Teras turn 1, WoW also works that same turn—but Knock Off is still the safer default because it works either way.

## Turn 2 onward

- If Chandelure has Terastallized and Safeguard is not up, **WoW it**.
- If it has not Terastallized, don't WoW; **Taunt** it or Knock Off its item.
- Bring **Excadrill** once sand is up. Click High Horsepower / Iron Head / Rock Slide. Protect when unsure.
- Keep Tyranitar healthy—sand is your speed control.

## Never do these

- Bring Gardevoir or Scizor into Chandelure.
- Click Electric moves while Raichu is alive.
- WoW Chandelure before it Teras.
- Chase KOs with Greninja into Scarf Chandelure.
- Waste WoW into Safeguard—kill Meowstic instead.

**Extra tip:** Sableye is your safe button. Fake Out cannot hit it, and Meowstic cannot Taunt it, so it always gets its support move. When in doubt, Knock Off the item or Taunt the setup Pokémon.

**One optional upgrade:** If Meowstic + Chandelure keeps beating you, try leading Sableye + Greninja in game 2—Dark Pulse Meowstic while Sableye Knocks Off Chandelure. But default to the sand core until you've seen his lead.
