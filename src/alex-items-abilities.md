# Alex items and abilities

Fast reference for the mechanics that decide this matchup. Alex's exact sets are unknown; these are the likely branches.

## Weather

| Ability         | Pokémon   | Effect                                                                                                 |
| --------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Drizzle**     | Politoed  | Sets rain for 5 turns, or 8 with Damp Rock. Water moves ×1.5; Fire moves ×0.5; enables Swift Swim.     |
| **Sand Stream** | Tyranitar | Sets sand for 5 turns. Rock-types get ×1.5 Special Defense; Rock/Ground/Steel are immune to sand chip. |

- On simultaneous entry, weather abilities activate in Speed order. Politoed (176) is faster than Tyranitar (158), so **Drizzle resolves first and Sand Stream resolves last: sand wins**.
- If Politoed switches in later, rain overwrites sand. Reset by switching Tyranitar out and back in, or KO Politoed.
- Sand chips non-Rock/Ground/Steel Pokémon for 1/16 per turn. Magic Guard ignores it.
- Tera Poison Tyranitar loses Rock's sand Special Defense boost and sand immunity, so it takes sand chip.
- Without sand, Excadrill loses Sand Rush and drops from 550 Speed to 275.

## Move and mechanic notes

### Follow Me

- **+2 priority**, so it beats Prankster Taunt/WoW.
- Redirects single-target moves to the user. Spread moves such as Rock Slide still hit both opponents.
- If Clefable uses Follow Me, it redirects the first single-target move, but Taunt on Clefable still lands afterward and stops the next Follow Me.
- If you Taunt Clefable's partner while Clefable uses Follow Me, the Taunt is redirected to Clefable.
- Do not use a single-target status move into Follow Me unless you are targeting Clefable itself.

### Taunt

- Lasts 3 turns. Stops status moves: Protect, Follow Me, recovery, Wish, Thunder Wave, Encore, screens, hazards, Leech Seed, Swords Dance.
- Does not remove already-active screens or already-set weather.
- Klefki's first screen still beats Taunt because Klefki is a faster Prankster.
- Taunt Clefable directly; Taunt its partner and Follow Me can redirect it to Clefable.

### Prankster and Dark types

- Prankster status moves get +1 priority.
- Dark-types are immune to opposing Prankster status moves that target them.
- Klefki's Prankster Thunder Wave **cannot hit Sableye, Tyranitar or Greninja**.
- It can hit Excadrill, Scizor and Gardevoir.
- Sableye's Prankster Taunt/Recover work normally because Alex has no Dark-types to block them. Foul Play and Knock Off are attacks, so they do not get Prankster priority.

### Burn

- Burn halves physical damage.
- **Fire-types cannot be burned.** Blaziken is Fire/Fighting, so **Will-O-Wisp fails on Blaziken**.
- If Blaziken Teras into a non-Fire type, it can be burned.
- Magic Guard prevents burn damage but not the Attack drop.
- Scald can burn Tyranitar and Excadrill. Tera Poison does not prevent burn.
- Sableye's recommended set uses **Foul Play**, not Will-O-Wisp.

### Foul Play

- Uses the **target's Attack** stat, not the user's.
- Punishes Swords Dance Blaziken and other high-Attack targets.
- Sableye's Foul Play does about 32–38% to +0 Blaziken and 71–83% to +2 Blaziken.
- Dark-type; Fairy-types resist it, so it is not a Clefable/Klefki breaker.

### Screens

- Reflect halves physical damage; Light Screen halves special damage.
- Light Clay makes them last 8 turns.
- Taunt stops new screens; it does not remove active ones.
- Knock Off Light Clay only shortens future screens, not the current ones.
- Brick Break or Psychic Fangs would remove screens, but Michael's recommended sets do not carry them.

### Hazards

- Stealth Rock and Spikes chip switch-ins.
- Rapid Spin removes them from Michael's side.
- Taunt Ferrothorn before it sets them.
- Magic Guard Clefable ignores hazard damage.

### Knock Off

- Removes the target's item; deals ×1.5 damage if the target has an item.
- Removing Damp Rock **before the next Drizzle** cuts future rain to 5 turns.
- Removing Damp Rock after rain starts does not shorten the current rain.
- Removing Light Clay after screens are up does not shorten the current screens.
- Removing Life Orb reduces Ludicolo/Blaziken damage by ×1.3.

### Protect

- +4 priority; blocks moves for the turn.
- Blaziken uses it to get a free Speed Boost; Ferrothorn/Ludicolo may use it to stall.
- Taunt stops future Protect, but Protect's +4 priority beats Taunt on the turn it is used.
- Do not give Blaziken free turns if it can Swords Dance instead.

### Encore

- Locks the target into its last move for 3 turns.
- Politoed can Encore Recover/Protect/Taunt.
- Do not spam Recover or Protect into Politoed.

### Fake Out

- +3 priority; flinches the target; only works on the user's first turn after switching in.
- Ludicolo can use it to stop Sableye's Taunt for one turn.
- The other Pokémon on the field still moves.

### Iron Barbs and Rocky Helmet

- Iron Barbs: 1/8 on contact. Rocky Helmet: 1/6 on contact. Both stack.
- Contact moves in Michael's sets: Fire Punch, Crunch, Superpower, Bullet Punch, U-turn, Knock Off, Close Combat, High Horsepower, Iron Head, Rapid Spin.
- Non-contact moves: Rock Slide, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam, Dark Pulse, Moonblast, Psychic, Psyshock, Focus Blast.
- Fire Punch is contact but is still the best way to hit Ferrothorn; accept the chip or use Rock Slide, Hydro Pump or Focus Blast when you need non-contact damage.

### Gyro Ball and Power Whip

- Gyro Ball gets stronger the slower Ferrothorn is relative to the target. Gardevoir is fast, so Gyro Ball is near maximum and OHKOs it.
- Power Whip is 120 base power, 85% accuracy, and OHKOs Greninja.
- Tera Poison Tyranitar resists Power Whip and is neutral to Gyro Ball.

### Tera

- Terastallizing changes the Pokémon's defensive type to the single Tera type.
- Same-type Tera keeps original STAB; different-type Tera loses the original STAB.
- Tera happens before the move, so status and type immunities check the new typing.
- One Tera per battle.
- Burn immunity follows the current typing: Fire-type Blaziken cannot be burned; a non-Fire Tera Blaziken can.
- Tera Poison Tyranitar is the primary defensive Tera. It resists Fighting/Fairy/Grass and is neutral to Water/Fire/Steel/Dark, but loses Rock STAB, sand Special Defense, sand immunity, and the Rock-type Fire resistance.
