## Team selection (4-of-6)

### Primary (recommended)

**Bring: Scizor, Excadrill, Tyranitar, Sableye**

Rationale: Sand from Tyranitar, Rapid Spin from Excadrill, pivoting/revenge from Scizor, and **Sableye Prankster disruption** (Taunt shuts down Blissey's Soft-Boiled; Wisp cripples physical threats; Knock Off strips utility).

### Contingencies

- **Shuckle/ hazards heavy:** Keep primary 4 (Excadrill spins). Sableye can Taunt Encore.
- **Slaking (Choice Band):** Keep primary 4. Sableye/Tyranitar tank/wait out Truant; Scizor/Greninja for revenge.
- **Need more special damage to break Veil/Ninetales/Blissey:** Swap **Scizor** → **Greninja** (bring Excadrill, Tyranitar, Sableye, Greninja). More immediate special pressure.
- **Need more Fairy/special bulk to muscle through:** Swap **Scizor** → **Gardevoir** (bring Excadrill, Tyranitar, Sableye, Gardevoir). Moonblast hits hard, good special sponge. Loses some pivot/momentum.

## Strategy

1. **Lead Tyranitar** to set Sand Stream turn 1 (chips Blissey/Amoonguss, hard to remove).
2. **Disrupt with Sableye**: Prankster Taunt Blissey (blocks Soft-Boiled), Taunt Weezing/Ninetales, or Will-O-Wisp physical threats. Come in on predicted switches.
3. **Hazard control**: If Shuckle sets Stealth Rock/Sticky Web, spin with Excadrill (Rapid Spin). Sableye can Taunt Encore to buy time.
4. **Handle Gas/Spore**: Avoid switching Scizor into Weezing while Neutralizing Gas is active (Technician disabled). Don't leave frail mons in front of Amoonguss (Spore). Sableye/Tyranitar/Excadrill are safer switches.
5. **Aurora Veil**: Play through Veil with chip (Sand) + sustained pressure; focus on breaking mons behind Veil rather than immediate KOs.
6. **Crack Blissey**: Taunt with Sableye first, then wear down with Tyranitar + chip/special pressure (Gardevoir can help).
7. **Pivoting/momentum**: Scizor's U-turn helps escape bad matchups and maintain pressure.
8. **Tera usage**: Save Teras for key breaks. Tera Sableye to Ghost (block Normal/Fighting), Tera Tyranitar to Dark (big Crunch), Tera Scizor to Steel (tank super effective), Tera Gardevoir to Fairy (boost Moonblast/tank Fairy), Tera Excadrill/Greninja as appropriate.
