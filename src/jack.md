   Standing rules for all 3 games
6. Taunt/Fake Out before setup resolves. Prankster +1 and Fake Out +3 both beat Follow Me, Thunder Wave, Dragon Dance and Shell Smash (all +0). This one sequencing habit beats his whole team structure.
7. Greninja answers Dragonite; Gardevoir never does. Moonblast into a full-HP Multiscale Dragonite leaves it alive at ~72% and procs Weakness Policy → sweep. Greninja's Ice Beam is an unconditional OHKO (~165% through Multiscale). If Greninja is gone, it needs sand chip or rocks first.
8. Prankster Thunder Wave flips any speed matchup. Key line: a +2 Blastoise hits 510 Speed — nothing outspeeds it — but para halves it to 255, and Greninja (377) then outspeeds it easily. Same for Scarf Gengar (525 → 262, below your whole team's fast half).
9. Never lead Gardevoir into an unscouted Gengar (games 2–3): it outspeeds (350 vs 284) and OHKOs with Sludge Bomb. Emergency protocol if Gengar appears game 1: Sableye TWs it before it moves (+1 beats its attack) → Gardevoir (284) then outspeeds the paralyzed Gengar (175) and Psychic OHKOs; if Hitmontop's Fake Out stops that TW, Tera Water Gardevoir immediately (Sludge Bomb drops to ~36%). Games 2–3: lead Tyranitar, which walls Gengar completely.
10. Stealth Rock early. 25% each entry on Togekiss + Dragonite, 12.5% on the rest, and it breaks Focus Sash (watch for Sash Gengar).
11. Careful Tyranitar eats ~64% from a Togekiss Dazzling Gleam — don't leave it in for two Gleams without Protect/Leftovers turns; Rock Slide back for 2×.
12. Chip wins this set: sand (6.25%/turn on 5 of his 6), rocks, paralysis, burns, Life Orb recoil on your side — in a 4v4 where his team is slow and disrupted, attrition plus Greninja cleanup is the consistent endgame.
